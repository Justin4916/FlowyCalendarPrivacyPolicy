Privacy Policy – Flowly Calendar

Effective Date: December 21, 2025

Flowly Calendar (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Google Classroom Data

  • If you choose to connect Google Classroom, we access your courses and assignments.
  • This data is used solely to display and organize your assignments and schedule within the app.
  • Google Classroom data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose outside the app’s core functionality.

Email Address

  • Your Google Classroom email address may be displayed in the app to indicate which account is currently connected.

2. How We Store Your Information

  • All assignment data and Google Classroom connection information are stored locally on your device.
  • Data is stored using Apple’s UserDefaults system or other secure on-device storage mechanisms.
  • We do not transmit, upload, or store your data on external servers.

3. Sharing and Third Parties

  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal information to third parties.
  • Google Classroom is a third-party service; we only access data that you explicitly authorize through Google’s authentication process.

4. Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

  • Flowly Calendar does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.
  • We do not track users across apps or websites.

5. Data Retention

  • Your assignment data remains on your device until you delete it, remove assignments, or disconnect your Google Classroom account.

6. Your Rights and Controls

  • You may disconnect your Google Classroom account at any time.
  • You may edit or delete local assignments directly within the app.

7. Children’s Privacy

  • Flowly Calendar does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Any data accessed through Google Classroom is provided by Google under the user’s authorization and subject to Google’s own privacy protections.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
